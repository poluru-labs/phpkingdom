Line Height

Vertical rhythm controls readability in paragraphs and headings.

line-height: 1.2
Tight leading suits large display headings but feels cramped in multi-line body copy.
line-height: 1.55
A comfortable default for paragraphs. Eyes track lines easily without excessive gaps.
line-height: 1.85
Looser leading helps small text, dense content, or accessibility-focused layouts.

Unitless values (e.g. 1.55) scale with font size; prefer them over fixed px.
