Text Align

Horizontal alignment for headings, prose, and UI blocks.

text-align: left
Default for LTR languages. Body copy starts at the left edge.
text-align: center
Hero titles and short callouts.
text-align: right
Prices, dates, or metadata columns.
text-align: justify
Justified text spreads lines to fill the width. Use sparingly on the web; uneven word spacing can hurt readability in narrow columns.

Avoid centered multi-line paragraphs for long reading. Left-aligned text is easiest to scan.
