Open Graph Social Meta Tags

Control how links appear when shared.

<meta property="og:title" content="HTML Tutorials | phpkingdom">
<meta property="og:description" content="Free HTML examples and tutorials.">
<meta property="og:type" content="website">
<meta property="og:url" content="https://phpkingdom.example/tutorials/html">
<meta property="og:image" content="https://placehold.co/1200x630/2a6f97/fff?text=phpkingdom">

Twitter cards often use twitter:card, twitter:title, and twitter:image.
