Forms Checklist

Review before publishing form markup.

  • Every input has a visible <label> with matching for / id.
  • Related radios/checkboxes grouped in <fieldset> with <legend>.
  • Choose method="get" for searches; post for sensitive or mutating actions.
  • Set enctype="multipart/form-data" when uploading files.
  • Use classic types: text, password, submit, reset, checkbox, radio, file, hidden, button.
  • Do not rely on placeholder alone instead of labels.
  • Provide clear submit button text (“Log in”, not “OK”).
  • Validate on the server; HTML attributes are hints only.
