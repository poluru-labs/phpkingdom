Readable page structure

Landmarks such as <header>, <main>, and <footer> help users navigate long pages.

  • One clear <h1> per page (already in this demo’s title area).
  • Short paragraphs and meaningful headings.
  • Sufficient color contrast between text and background (test with a contrast checker).
  • Do not rely on color alone to convey errors—use text labels too.
