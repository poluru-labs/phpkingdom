Getting started

Do I need prior coding experience?
No. Start with the basics chapters, then work through exercises at your own pace. HTML structure is taught before advanced topics.
Are the templates free to use?
Yes for learning and personal projects. Credit to phpkingdom is appreciated. Commercial redistribution requires permission.
Which browser should I use?
Any modern browser works. Use View Source to inspect examples — that habit matters more than the browser brand.

Templates & exercises

Why minimal CSS on template pages?
So you focus on HTML landmarks, headings, lists, tables, and forms. Add your own styles after the markup feels correct.
Can I submit exercise solutions for review?
Email a link or ZIP to mail.polurus@gmail.com. Community review batches run monthly.

Store & billing

How are digital products delivered?
Download links arrive by email within a few minutes of purchase. Check spam if you do not see them.
