Our mission

phpkingdom teaches web development through small, readable examples. We believe learners grasp layout faster when HTML structure is clear and CSS stays minimal.

What we offer

  • Hands-on exercises with problem/solution pairs
  • Classic page templates for practice and reference
  • Notes on PHP and practical backend topics
