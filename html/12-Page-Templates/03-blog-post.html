Why structure comes before styling

By Subrahmanyam Poluru · July 19, 2026 · Tags: HTML, beginners

When I teach at phpkingdom, I ask students to disable CSS in the browser. If the page still reads logically, the HTML is doing its job.

Use landmarks early

Wrap recurring regions in semantic elements:

  • header — site or article introduction
  • nav — primary navigation links
  • main — unique page content
  • footer — copyright, contact, secondary links

Headings tell the story

One h1 per page. Section titles step down levels (h2, then h3) without skipping.

Good markup is documentation for the next developer — often yourself, six months later.
