Solution 10 — cy.intercept Stub

Intercept GET /api/todos and return a fixture.

Solution

cy.intercept('GET', '/api/todos', { fixture: 'todos.json' }).as('todos');
cy.visit('/todos');
cy.wait('@todos');
cy.get('[data-cy=todo]').should('have.length.greaterThan', 0);
