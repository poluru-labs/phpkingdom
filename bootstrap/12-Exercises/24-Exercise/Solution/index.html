Solution 24 — Spinner Button

Put a spinner-border spinner-border-sm inside a disabled button with visually-hidden text.

<button class="btn btn-primary" type="button" disabled><span class="spinner-border spinner-border-sm" aria-hidden="true"></span><span class="visually-hidden" role="status">Loading...</span> Loading...</button>
